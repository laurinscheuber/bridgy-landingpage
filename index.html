Frequently Asked Questions

How does Bridgy save 65% of handoff time?

Research by Chen et al. (2024) shows manual handoffs are "fragile and error-prone". Bridgy eliminates manual token copying, CSS updates, and design-code inconsistencies through automation.

Which frameworks does Bridgy support?

Bridgy works with Angular, React, and Vue. It generates framework-specific tests and exports design tokens compatible with CSS, SCSS, and Tailwind CSS.

Is Bridgy compatible with our CI/CD pipeline?

Yes! Bridgy integrates directly with GitLab and GitHub, automatically creating commits and merge requests when design tokens are updated in Figma.

What scientific research backs Bridgy?

Multiple studies confirm manual design-to-code workflows are inefficient: Chen (2024) on fragile handoffs, Pacheco (2022) on time consumption, and Leinonen & Roto (2023) on tool fragmentation.

How much does Bridgy cost?

Bridgy's core is open source and free. We offer premium features for enterprise teams including advanced integrations and priority support.

Can Bridgy handle large design systems?

Absolutely. Bridgy is built for scalability and has been tested with design systems containing 100+ components and thousands of design tokens.
